Privacy Policy
Website
Our website address is https://imin2uk.com.
Comments
When visitors leave comments on our site, we may collect the data shown in the comments form, as well as the visitor’s IP address, browser type, and other related information. This is primarily for spam detection and prevention.
An anonymised string (also known as a hash) created from the visitor’s email address may be provided to the Gravatar service. The Gravatar service privacy policy is available at: https://automattic.com/privacy/
If a visitor’s comment is approved, their profile picture may be publicly visible alongside the comment.
Media
If visitors upload images or videos to our website, any embedded location data (EXIF GPS) included in the files will also be uploaded. Other visitors or site administrators may be able to download and extract location data from these files.
Cookies
If you leave a comment on our site, you may opt to save your name, email address, and website in cookies. These will be stored for one year.
When you visit our login page, we will set a temporary cookie to determine if your browser accepts cookies. This cookie contains no personal data and is discarded when you close your browser.
When you log in, we will also set up cookies to save your login details and your screen display preferences. Login cookies last for two days, and screen options cookies last for one year. If you select “Remember Me”, your login will persist for two weeks. When you log out, login cookies will be removed.
If you edit or publish a post, an additional cookie will be saved in your browser. This cookie contains no personal data and simply indicates the post ID of the content you have just edited.
Embedded Content from Other Websites
Articles on our site may include embedded content, such as videos, images, or articles. Embedded content from other websites behaves in exactly the same way as if the visitor had visited the other website directly.
If you are logged into an account on our site and interact with embedded content from another website, that website may collect data about you and enable tracking or monitoring of your interaction.
Data Retention and Your Rights
If you leave a comment, the comment and its metadata are retained indefinitely.
For users who register on our site, we store the personal information they provide in their profile. All users can view, edit, or delete their personal information at any time. Website administrators can also view and edit this information.
If you have an account on our site, or have left comments containing personal information, you may request to receive an exported file of the personal data we hold about you, or request that we erase it. However, we may be obliged to retain certain information for legal or regulatory purposes.
