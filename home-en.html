Visa Application Process with IMIN2UK
01.
Initial Free Consultation
For first-time enquiries, we assess your current circumstances and requirements, then advise on the most suitable visa options and typical costs.
02.
Service Information
Once you decide to proceed with Imin2UK, we provide a tailored document checklist for your chosen visa category along with a detailed cost invoice.
03.
Application & Submission
As soon as we receive your required documents, service fee, and any applicable costs, we submit your application to the Home Office without delay.
04.
Online and Visa Centre Procedures
You may need to pay the Home Office fee online yourself, or attend a UK Visa Application Centre to register your biometrics (fingerprints, etc.).
05.
Confirmation & Updates
Once your application is submitted to the Home Office, Imin2UK provides you with regular progress updates in a timely manner.
06.
Visa Approval & Arrival
When your visa is granted, usually in the form of an eVisa, you will be guided on how to use your Share Code. You can then begin the life you’ve planned.
