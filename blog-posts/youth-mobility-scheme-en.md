<p>The UK Youth Mobility Scheme (YMS), commonly known as a working holiday visa, is one of the most straightforward visa categories in the UK immigration system. This visa is not open to all nationalities—it is available only to citizens of selected countries, including Australia, New Zealand, Canada, Japan, and South Korea. The age limits and annual quotas vary between participating countries.</p><img class="blog-body-image" alt="" src="/images/Imin2UK-home-service-3-1536x1024-p-1080.webp" width="auto" height="auto" loading="auto"><h2>Eligibility and Age Requirements</h2><p>For South Korean nationals, the YMS visa is open to applicants aged 18 to 35. Dependants are not permitted to accompany the main applicant. If siblings wish to come together, each must apply individually. The upper age limit of 35 applies to nationals of South Korea, Australia, Canada, and New Zealand. For other eligible nationalities, the age limit is 30.</p><h2>Annual Quota</h2><p>As of 2026, 5,000 places are allocated annually for South Korean nationals, meaning up to 5,000 applicants can receive a YMS visa each year. The following year's quota is determined through negotiations between the UK and South Korean governments. If no new agreement is reached, the quota may be reduced. It is therefore advisable to apply as early as possible if you are planning to use this route.</p><h2>When to Apply</h2><p>You can apply for the YMS visa up to six months before your intended date of entry to the UK. As the process involves not only the online application but also booking and attending an appointment at a visa application centre, it is recommended to start preparing well in advance. If your situation is urgent, you may pay a priority fee when booking your visa centre appointment to speed up the process, though planning ahead is always preferable.</p><h2>Required Documents</h2><p>You must prepare the following documents before submitting your visa application.</p><p><strong>Passport:</strong> You need a valid passport with at least two years of remaining validity to cover the full two-year duration of the YMS visa. If your passport is close to expiring, it is best to renew it before applying.</p><p><strong>Financial Evidence:</strong> You must hold at least £2,530 (or the equivalent in your local currency) in a bank account in your name for a minimum of 28 consecutive days before the date of your application. Obtain a bank statement clearly showing the required period and balance. This amount is separate from the visa application fee and health surcharge.</p><p><strong>Tuberculosis (TB) Test:</strong> Applicants from certain countries, including South Korea, must take a TB test at an approved clinic and obtain a clear result certificate before applying. Check the <a href="https://www.gov.uk/tb-test-visa" target="_blank">UK government website</a> for the list of approved clinics in your country.</p><h2>Online Application and Fees</h2><p>The visa application is submitted online through the <a href="https://www.gov.uk/youth-mobility/apply" target="_blank">UK government website</a>. Make sure you have a credit or debit card that supports international payments, as some locally issued cards may have overseas transactions blocked by default.</p><p>Two fees are payable during the online application. First, the YMS visa application fee of £340. Second, the Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS) of £1,552 for two years. These figures are as of April 2026 and are subject to change. Note that these fees are separate from the £2,530 financial evidence requirement.</p><h2>Visa Application Centre (VFS Global) Appointment</h2><p>After completing the online application, you must book an appointment at a UK visa application centre operated by VFS Global. During the booking process, you will need to enter your personal details and the unique reference number issued at the end of your online application.</p><p>At your appointment, bring all required documents—including your bank statement, TB test certificate, and passport—and provide your biometric information (fingerprints and photograph). You may upload scanned copies of your documents online before your visit, but it is strongly recommended to bring the originals as well. Additional document requirements will be outlined during the booking process.</p><h2>Visa Issuance and Entry to the UK</h2><p>Once all steps are completed, your YMS visa will be issued as an eVisa within approximately ten days to three or four weeks. Rather than a physical sticker in your passport, the visa exists digitally. You will receive instructions on how to access your eVisa and generate a share code.</p><p>Although your passport and visa details are linked digitally, allowing you to use eGates at UK airports, it is recommended that first-time YMS entrants present their visa confirmation documents and share code to a border officer in person. This is a precaution in case the digital records are temporarily unavailable. After your initial entry, you may use eGates freely for the remainder of your visa period.</p><h2>Length of Stay</h2><p>The YMS visa is valid for two years and cannot be extended. An extension of one year is available only to holders of Australian, New Zealand, or Canadian passports. The YMS visa does not directly lead to Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR), but during your stay you may have the opportunity to switch to a longer-term visa route such as the Skilled Worker visa.</p><h2>Application Checklist</h2><p>Here is a summary of the key steps to prepare for your YMS visa application:</p><p>1. Check your passport validity (at least two years remaining)<br>2. Maintain at least £2,530 in your bank account for 28 consecutive days and obtain a bank statement<br>3. Take a TB test at an approved clinic and obtain a clear result certificate<br>4. Ensure your credit or debit card supports international payments<br>5. Complete the online application and pay the fees (visa fee £340 + IHS £1,552)<br>6. Book and attend a VFS Global visa application centre appointment for biometric enrolment and document submission</p><hr><p>While the process may seem complex at first, working through the checklist step by step makes it quite manageable. The YMS visa is straightforward enough that most applicants can successfully complete the process on their own. However, if you need professional assistance, we recommend working with an immigration agency authorised by the Immigration Advice Authority (IAA) under the UK Home Office, such as Imin2UK.</p>
