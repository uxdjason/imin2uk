<p>In this guide, we introduce a unique visa category: the High Potential Individual (HPI) visa. The HPI visa allows recent graduates from top-ranking global universities outside the UK to live and work freely in the UK without a sponsor. The visa is granted for 2 years for bachelor's or master's degree holders, and 3 years for PhD graduates.</p><img class="blog-body-image" alt="UK High Potential Individual (HPI) Visa" src="/images/imin2uk-hpi-visa.webp" width="auto" height="auto" loading="auto"><h2>What is the HPI Visa?</h2><p>You can think of the HPI visa as the overseas university equivalent of the <a href="/graduate-visa-en/">Graduate Visa</a> (which is for those who studied at UK universities). Even if you graduated from a prestigious university outside the UK, you can apply for this visa to work freely in the UK without needing an employer to sponsor you, provided your university is on the approved list.</p><p>To be eligible, the institution that awarded your degree must appear on the UK government's Global Universities List for the year you graduated. This list is updated annually, so whether your university is included may vary from year to year. If you have graduated with a bachelor's, master's, or PhD from an eligible global university, you may be able to apply.</p><h2>Annual Quota and Renewal</h2><p>The HPI visa is subject to an annual quota. As of November 2025, the limit is set at 8,000 visas per year. This quota is global, meaning applicants from all over the world are competing for these available spots. As a result, depending on when you apply, the quota may be exhausted.</p><p>The quota is renewed annually on 1 November. If you attempt to apply but find the quota has been met, you may have the option to wait and apply after the quota resets in November. However, you must still meet the requirement to apply within 5 years of your graduation.</p><h2>Application Timeline</h2><p>You must apply for the HPI visa within 5 years of the date your degree was awarded. For example, if you graduated on 20 February 2022, you must apply before 20 February 2027. Despite this 5-year window, it is highly recommended not to delay your application unnecessarily. Immigration rules can change, and there is no guarantee the HPI visa route will remain open indefinitely under its current terms. If you are eligible now, it is best to apply as soon as possible.</p><h2>Eligible Universities List by Year</h2><p>To qualify for the HPI visa, your university must be on the list corresponding to the month and year you were awarded your degree. The lists cover graduates awarded qualifications between 1 November of the stated year and 31 October of the following year. You can check the official UK government lists for each period using the links below:</p><ul><li><a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/high-potential-individual-visa-global-universities-list/high-potential-individual-visa-global-universities-list-2020" target="_blank">2020 List (Graduation: 1 Nov 2020 - 31 Oct 2021)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/high-potential-individual-visa-global-universities-list/high-potential-individual-visa-global-universities-list-2021" target="_blank">2021 List (Graduation: 1 Nov 2021 - 31 Oct 2022)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/high-potential-individual-visa-global-universities-list/high-potential-individual-visa-global-universities-list-2022" target="_blank">2022 List (Graduation: 1 Nov 2022 - 31 Oct 2023)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/high-potential-individual-visa-global-universities-list/high-potential-individual-visa-global-universities-list-2023" target="_blank">2023 List (Graduation: 1 Nov 2023 - 31 Oct 2024)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/high-potential-individual-visa-global-universities-list/high-potential-individual-visa-global-universities-list-2024" target="_blank">2024 List (Graduation: 1 Nov 2024 - 31 Oct 2025)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/high-potential-individual-visa-global-universities-list/high-potential-individual-visa-global-universities-list-2025" target="_blank">2025 List (Graduation: 1 Nov 2025 - 31 Oct 2026)</a></li></ul><p>Checking these lists carefully based on your graduation date is the very first and most crucial step in preparing your application.</p><h2>Required Documents and Procedures</h2><p>Once you have confirmed your university is on the list and you are within the 5-year limit, you can begin preparing your application. Since the HPI visa is usually applied for from outside the UK, there are several steps involved.</p><p>Validation of Degree<br>You must have your degree validated by <a href="https://qls.ecctis.com/" target="_blank">Ecctis</a> to prove it is equivalent to a UK bachelor's, master's, or PhD. This is done online, and as of March 2026, the fee is £252 including VAT. Validating your qualification takes time, so you should start this process first.</p><p>Tuberculosis (TB) Test<br>If you are applying from a country where TB testing is required, you must take a TB test at an approved clinic and obtain a clear certificate. If you are already in the UK on another visa and are switching to the HPI visa, this is generally not required. It is efficient to book your TB test while waiting for your Ecctis results.</p><p>English Language Requirement<br>You must prove your knowledge of English. From 8 January 2026, the standard was raised to CEFR level B2. Currently, you need to score <strong>IELTS for UKVI at least 5.5 in each component</strong> to be eligible. This is equivalent to the standard required for a standard UK Student visa.</p><p>Financial Evidence<br>You must show that you can support yourself financially upon arriving in the UK. You need to have at least £1,270 in your bank account for 28 consecutive days before the date you apply. You will need a bank statement or an official letter from your bank clearly showing your name, the balance, and the dates.</p><h2>Online Application and Fees</h2><p>After gathering your documents, you will submit your visa application online. You will need to pay two main fees during this process.</p><p>First is the HPI visa application fee of £880. Second is the Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS) of £1,035 per year. This means you will pay £2,070 for a 2-year visa (bachelor's/master's), or £3,105 for a 3-year visa (PhD). If you are using a card issued outside the UK, ensure that your bank allows international transactions before proceeding.</p><h2>Visiting the Visa Application Centre (VFS Global)</h2><p>Upon completing the online application, you will receive a reference number. You must use this to book an appointment at a VFS Global visa application centre in your country.</p><p>You must bring your passport and all supporting documents to your appointment. Where possible, scan and upload your documents online beforehand, but you must still bring the original documents with you. At the centre, your biometric information (fingerprints and photograph) will be taken. Do not book any travel until you have received your visa.</p><h2>Visa Issuance and Entry</h2><p>Visas are typically processed within 3 to 4 weeks. In most cases, the visa is issued digitally as an eVisa rather than a physical sticker (vignette) in your passport. You will access your visa information via your UKVI account, where you can generate a share code to prove your immigration status when required. You should familiarise yourself with how to generate a share code before travelling.</p><h2>Dependants</h2><p>Your partner and children can apply to join or stay with you in the UK as your dependants. Each family member must submit their own application and pay the application fee and the Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS) individually.</p><h2>Application Checklist</h2><p>Here is a summary of the required steps for an HPI visa application:</p><p>1. Check the official lists to confirm your university was eligible in your graduation year<br>2. Ensure you are applying within 5 years of your graduation date<br>3. Apply for degree validation via Ecctis (fee £252)<br>4. Obtain a clear TB test certificate from an approved clinic (if applicable)<br>5. Prove English language proficiency (e.g., IELTS for UKVI 5.5 in all components, CEFR B2)<br>6. Prove financial maintenance: at least £1,270 held for 28 days with an official bank statement<br>7. Complete the online application and pay the fees (visa fee £880 + IHS £2,070 or £3,105)<br>8. Book and attend your appointment at a VFS Global visa application centre</p><hr><p>The HPI visa offers a fantastic opportunity to live and build a career in the UK without needing an employer sponsor. If your university is on the eligible list, you essentially have access to the same benefits as the UK Graduate Visa, without the need to have studied in the UK. This represents an excellent and accessible pathway for top global talent to work in the UK.</p><p>However, it is important to remember that, like the Graduate Visa and the Youth Mobility Scheme, the HPI visa <strong>cannot be extended and does not lead directly to settlement (ILR).</strong> If you find living in the UK rewarding and wish to continue your career here, you can switch to a Skilled Worker visa from within the UK, provided you secure a job with an approved sponsor before your HPI visa expires.</p><p>If you require assistance with your HPI visa application or any other UK immigration matters, or if you simply have questions about the process, do not hesitate to contact Imin2UK.
